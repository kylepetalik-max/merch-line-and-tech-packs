## Hard holds — read before sending anything to a factory

**License hold — Sur-Ron / Talaria.** `DCL-SURRON` and `DCL-TALARIA` use OEM wordmarks and vehicle likenesses. No factory-ready pack, no PO, no sellable PDP, and no paid ads using those marks until there is written brand permission or a license. An OEM-free generic wrap would be a new design brief, not these two SKUs.

**Helmets, goggles, gloves, boots, chest armor: untested.** Mockups for `MXHM`, `GOG`, `MXG`, `MXB`, `BAR`, `RDH`, `RDJ`, `RDB` may show DOT / ECE / CE marks. Treat every one of those as a **graphic, not a certification**. Nothing here has been tested by a factory or a notified body. Do not print a cert on a hangtag, listing, or PDP, and do not sell these as riding safety gear. The `BAR` mockup's red CE stickers must be stripped or validated.

**Fashion outerwear is not PPE.** `BMB` (bomber) and `WND` (windbreaker) are streetwear. They are not the `RDJ` riding jacket and carry no armor or abrasion claim.

**Missing art.** 17 catalogue image refs 403'd. Women's (3) and kids (3) SKUs need new mockups before packs; the trucker cap and snapback currently share one composite photo; `TRK-BLK-GLD` and `BEN-MULTI` have no usable art at all.

Prices listed are intended list prices, not quotes — get mill GSM, embroidery vs AOP, and trim pricing before locking any of them.
