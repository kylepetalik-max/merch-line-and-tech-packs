## 8. QC notes (DRAFT)

- **No CE, EN 17092, DOT, ECE, or impact-armor language** on hangtag, care label, or PDP. Fashion bomber. Mockup is a design still. Any safety mark would be an untested claim.
- Rib shade: mustard gold, not the same as metallic gold thread. Two golds — call them **rib gold** vs **metal/thread gold** so QC does not reject a shade match that was never meant to match.
- Zip: gold finish vs rib gold will not be identical. Approve a trims board.
- Back graffiti: no cracking on satin; use compatible ink. Embroidery must not punch/tear satin — test needle and backing.
- Sleeve patch: square, no twist, merrow even.
- Quilt lining: no cold spots, even baffle.
- Only rear mockup — **front, interior, and wear photos are missing.** Do not approve bulk until a proto is photographed front + open.

Not PPE. Not a helmet. Not chest armor.
