## 8. QC notes (DRAFT)

- Registration: gold gear centered under tag, slogan centered under gear. Max shift 1.5 mm.
- Metallic coverage opaque on black and heather; no mesh shine-through.
- Cream != white. Two lab dips.
- Heather: no streaky gold; test foil vs metallic ink (foil may crack; ink may look dull).
- Neck stretch recover. No rib bite.
- Print hand: no boardy plastisol brick on a $65 tee — prefer waterbase + metallic highlight, or thin plastisol.
- Wash 3x: no crack on graffiti edges, no gold flake.
- Side-seam twist <=1 cm after wash.
- Missing `_2 _3 _4` images: do not write PDP copy that claims back/side views you do not have.
