## 8. QC notes (DRAFT)

- Sleeve script: gold fills **inside** the white letterforms. Misregister >1.0 mm is a reject.
- Vertical alignment: script follows sleeve grain, not twisted toward inseam.
- Left chest not drifting onto CF or armhole.
- Cuff rib recover; sleeve print must not sit on the rib.
- Same gold formula as TEE-GRAF / SHT.
- Wash 3x: no flake on metallic fills.
- Only one mockup — no back/side proof. Do not claim back art in PDP.
